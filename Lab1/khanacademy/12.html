Recipe #1(Tiramisu)

  • Time: 8-10 hours
  • Serves:
Ingredients Quantity
chicken eggs 6
mascarpone cream cheese 500g
sugar 150g
savoiardi cookies 250g
espresso coffee 300ml
tbsp cocoa powder 1-2
cognac (optional) 50g

Step 1: Prepare coffee.
Step 2: Whip mascarpone with sugar.
Step 3: Whip eggs or cream separately.
Step 4: Dip ladyfinger cookies.
Step 5:Layer cookies and cream.
Step 6:Dust the top with cocoa powder.
Step 7:Chill in the fridge 4–6 hours.
